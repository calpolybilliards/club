About Us

The Cal Poly Billiards Club is a student-run organization at California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo. We provide a space for Cal Poly students to improve and play billiards games, such as 8-ball, 9-ball, and 10-ball pool. No experience required to join, welcoming all casual, beginner, and competitive players.

For experienced players looking for more than casual play, consider joining our billiards team. We compete in local central coast APA leages and against other college clubs. Reach out for more details!

Events

The Cal Poly Billiards Club meets once a week at the Mustang Langs in the University Union from 6-9 PM on Wednesdays. Join us for casual games and food free of charge! No prior experience required to play!

Stay tuned for tournaments later this year at Hot Shots Billiards in Pismo Beach!
